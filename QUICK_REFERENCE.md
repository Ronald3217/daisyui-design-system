# Quick Reference Tables

> Quick overview of common classes, sizes, colors, and responsive breakpoints.

| Component | XS | SM | MD (Default) | LG | XL |
|-----------|----|----|-------------|----|----|
| **Button** | `btn-xs` | `btn-sm` | `btn-md` | `btn-lg` | `btn-xl` |
| **Input** | `input-xs` | `input-sm` | `input-md` | `input-lg` | `input-xl` |
| **Select** | `select-xs` | `select-sm` | `select-md` | `select-lg` | `select-xl` |
| **Textarea** | `textarea-xs` | `textarea-sm` | `textarea-md` | `textarea-lg` | `textarea-xl` |
| **Badge** | `badge-xs` | `badge-sm` | `badge-md` | `badge-lg` | `badge-xl` |
| **Avatar** | `w-6` | `w-8` | `w-12` | `w-16` | `w-24` |
| **Checkbox** | `checkbox-xs` | `checkbox-sm` | `checkbox-md` | `checkbox-lg` | - |
| **Radio** | `radio-xs` | `radio-sm` | `radio-md` | `radio-lg` | - |
| **Toggle** | `toggle-xs` | `toggle-sm` | `toggle-md` | `toggle-lg` | - |
